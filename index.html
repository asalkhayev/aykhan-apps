How funding works

Each school shows one of these labels and what he would pay.

FREENo tuition at all. Hungary's scholarship also pays a monthly stipend and housing help.
FULL RIDEA scholarship pays everything for the full degree. You win it separately from admission.
FULL NEEDThe school pays whatever the family cannot. The family sends income documents (CSS Profile). Low income means little or nothing to pay. Admission is separate and very selective.
STATE PROGRAMFull price, but the Dövlət Proqramı has paid for bachelor's students here before. If he gets in and the state accepts him, the state pays tuition and living costs.
PARTIALA scholarship covers part. The amount comes with the offer, so the cost is unknown until then.
LOW COSTTuition is about €5k a year or less even without a scholarship.
PAYFull price with no known route to cover it. Only worth applying to if a scholarship turns up.

Need-blind means his ability to pay does not affect admission. Need-aware means it can. For internationals only Harvard, Yale, Princeton, MIT, Amherst, Bowdoin and Columbia are need-blind. Every other US school with full aid is need-aware.

What the rules say

  • 500 places a year in total, and no more than 25% (about 125) for bachelor's.
  • Top-10 rule: with an admission letter from a university in the top 10 of QS or Times Higher Education, any field is accepted.
  • The state pays tuition, monthly living costs, health insurance, travel and visa.
  • He applies after he has an admission letter. The 2023 call closed 14 July, so expect a summer deadline.
  • Language: a C1 certificate. IELTS 7.0 should meet this, confirm on the program page.
  • Entrance: the DİM exam, at least 400 points for technical fields, unless exempt. A high SAT can replace it, and an olympiad medal exempts him from the exam and the interview.
